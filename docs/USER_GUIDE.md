# Rivet — User Guide

The manual for the people running their business on Rivet. Task-based; every section is
the short version of "how do I…". In the app, ask Bolt (bottom of the sidebar, or More →
Ask Bolt on a phone) — it answers these questions and looks up your own numbers.

## Getting started

Sign in at [getrivet.ai](https://getrivet.ai) with Google or email. Onboarding creates
your company; the dashboard's Getting-started checklist walks the rest: price book,
first quote, Stripe, logo, review links. Dismiss it any time — it stays in Settings.

## The price book

Everything Rivet drafts is priced from **your** price book — never invented.

- **Bring your own**: Price book → Import — CSV, or a photo of an old quote or rate
  sheet. Imports merge by name with the starter book; your version wins.
- **Starter book**: pre-filled for your trade. Archive any or all of it (select all →
  archive) — archived items never appear in drafts.
- **Edit prices inline**; every change records who and when, and can be reverted.
- **Labour hours per item** is what makes scheduling honest — the calendar knows how
  long a job really takes because the items say so.
- Technicians see the book only if you switch it on for them (Settings → Team).

## Quotes

**New quote** → describe the job in plain words → **Smart draft**. Line items arrive
priced from your book. If the description is too vague to price, you get a question
with tappable answers instead of a guess. Below the lines, "goes with this job"
suggests items your own history pairs together — one tap to add.

Add rows by searching the book or writing your own; discounts are a line with a
negative price. Photos attach from the job page. Save, then **Send** — the customer
gets a link, no login or app on their side.

**Price-match / revise**: edit the lines on the job page and re-send. The link stays
the same; the customer sees the current version.

## Signature and terms

Your terms (Settings → Company) show on every quote. The customer types their name to
accept; Rivet records signer, time, IP, and the exact terms text they agreed to. The
**Acceptance record** card on the job page holds it, and the PDF carries the signature
block — pull it up any time a customer asks "what did I agree to?".

## Scheduling

A won quote becomes a job with one tap. The calendar suggests slots inside your working
hours (Settings) sized by the quote's labour hours. Drag to reschedule, or use the
job's Reschedule button — same result.

**Repeating work** (weekly cleans, monthly maintenance): job → Details → Repeats →
weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, or **Custom** (every N days/weeks/months). Each visit
spawns as its own scheduled job with the same line items and tech.
"Email the invoice automatically" makes each visit bill itself.

## Invoicing and getting paid

Completed job → **Convert to invoice** → Send. With Stripe connected (Integrations),
customers pay online — card or bank transfer — and the invoice marks itself paid.
Without Stripe, the invoice shows your contact details for check/cash/transfer and you
record the payment by hand on the job page. Both paths land in the same books.

## QuickBooks

Integrations → QuickBooks → connect once. Every invoice and payment posts itself —
real items (not a generic line), tax as a proper liability, totals matching to the
cent. Sync failures surface on the Integrations card; nothing silently drops.

## Reviews

Settings → Company → paste your Google and/or Facebook review links. On a completed
job, **Ask for a review** emails the customer your links. It's one tap, and Rivet
never asks on your behalf without you.

## Team

Settings → Team → Invite. Roles: **owner** (everything), **admin** (everything but
billing/deletion), **office** (pipeline, scheduling, invoicing), **technician** (their
jobs, no money screens). Technicians see revenue nowhere. Catalog editing is a
per-person toggle.

Internal notes on a job are private to your team; @mention a teammate and they're
emailed — reply goes to the author, not the customer.

## Switching from another product

Import → pick Jobber, Housecall Pro, or Joist. The wizard shows where their export
button is and takes the file from there — customers, and your price book via CSV or
photo.

## Your plan

Two plans, everything included, no add-ons: **Solo $39/mo**, **Team $99/mo**.
14-day free trial, card up front, nothing charged until day 14. Cancel from Settings →
Billing — you keep access to the end of the period. Rivet won't spam your customers or
your inbox.

## Help

**Ask Bolt** — bottom of the sidebar (More → Ask Bolt on a phone). It answers how-to
questions and your own business questions ("what does my day look like?", "any overdue
invoices?"). It looks things up; it never changes anything.

**Rather talk to a human?** — same panel, "Email us". A person reads it and replies to
your email.
